# Physical-iPhone App Review walkthrough

Status: recording is deferred at the user’s request. This is a runbook, not evidence that the steps passed. Use the next review build only after its matching backend is deployed. The new answer/photo reporting and member blocking/unblocking controls are pending that build; build 16 cannot demonstrate them. Exact new safety-control labels must be checked against the finished build.

Use only a disposable family and test accounts. Suggested names: `Review Family`, `Review Owner`, `Review Member`. Prepare two dedicated accessible email inboxes and a harmless self-created photo (for example, a drawing reading “Review test”). Two physical iPhones are simplest. If only one is available, enroll the owner’s email first, generate the member invitation immediately before switching, then enroll the member’s email and alternate accounts through email login. Never sign out of an account before confirming a working recovery method. Do not use or delete the real family.

Before recording, note the physical device model, iOS version, TestFlight build number, backend URL, local time and Japan time. Check internet access and that the current question is published and unanswered for both test accounts. Questions publish between 09:00 and 19:00 Japan time; if the app displays a future publication time, wait for it. Do not alter server/device clocks or manufacture a published question. Keep mailbox passwords and recovery keys out of the recording.

1. **Start and purpose.** Show the installed TestFlight build and launch the real app. Briefly explain that it is free, Japanese-language, private family sharing around one daily question, with large controls for older relatives. No subscription, purchase, paid tier or payment screen exists; there is no paid flow to demonstrate.

2. **Fresh organizer setup.** Choose `新しく家族をつくる`, enter the disposable organizer/family names and tap `この名前で次へ`. Show that no password, phone or email verification is required to begin. Complete `あなたのしるし` by drawing a real line/shape, then `このしるしを使う`. Show `家族の準備` and continue through `使い方を見てはじめる`. Demonstrate that notification permission is optional. The upcoming safety build must also show any new terms/conduct acceptance actually present in its setup; this is pending verification.

3. **Email enrollment — real inbox proof required.** On the owner account open `あなた → 設定 → 機種変更・データの保存 → メールを登録する`. Enter the dedicated owner inbox and tap `確認メールを送る`. Receive the real six-digit email code and verify it in the app. Show the enrolled status. Record delivery/verification success or the actual failure; this flow has not yet been validated by real inbox evidence. Check spam/resend only through normal app behavior. Do not substitute a developer/universal code.

4. **Email login.** Sign out using the app’s normal control and choose `メールでログイン`. Use the enrolled address, receive a new real email code and finish login. Verify the same owner name, personal mark and family return. This establishes returning-login behavior rather than merely showing an enrollment screen.

5. **Second member and mandatory mark.** From the organizer’s family settings add `Review Member` and generate a setting invitation. On the second iPhone choose `家族の番号を入力`, enter the six-digit code within ten minutes, confirm that member, and draw their own distinct mark. Show both members in the same family and two cells in its answer-status indicator. Enroll/login with the second dedicated inbox as in steps 3–4 if switching accounts on one device.

6. **Text and photo UGC.** On `Review Member`, open today’s available question using `回答する`, enter a harmless test answer and attach the self-created photo through the real photo picker. Review then send. Show that published answers have no edit/delete buttons. On `Review Owner`, let the family feed update and open the member’s photo full screen. Demonstrate horizontal photo paging if more than one photo was intentionally attached, and swipe down/`閉じる` to dismiss. Do not use another person’s photos or private recordings.

7. **Two-member interaction.** On the owner account like the member’s answer and add a harmless comment. On the member account show the comment and add a reply, then return to the owner. Show `あなた` history/search and `家族` selection. If demonstrating audio, use a short self-recorded phrase and the actual microphone/speech permissions; typing must remain usable when permissions are declined.

8. **Reporting — pending new build.** From the owner account report the other member’s test answer/photo using the new control and the “その他” reason. If the finished UI offers an explanation field, use a truthful test explanation (for example, “App Review safety-flow test; harmless test content”); the current safety-screen implementation has reason choices only. Also demonstrate reporting the other member’s comment if Apple requires that surface. Show confirmation. Coordinate the report with the app operator’s new moderation workflow; a “report received” screen alone does not prove operator review. Do not invent prohibited material merely to make a report.

9. **Block and unblock — pending new build.** From the owner account block `Review Member` through the finished safety control. Show the confirmation and the actual resulting hiding/restriction behavior. Restart/refresh and check it persists; the owner’s own history and unrelated settings must remain intact. Open the blocked-member management control, unblock that member, and verify unreported family content/interaction behavior returns as designed; reported content must stay hidden. Run this step before step 8, or retain separate unreported test content so restoration can actually be demonstrated. Do not claim a broader effect than the implemented server and UI enforce.

10. **Notifications and settings.** If notification permission is enabled, show normal category/mute controls and, only if an eligible event occurs, its real notification and tap destination. Do not bypass quiet hours or reuse the consumed one-time production test. Build 16 already has separate evidence for one physical notification/today-question tap; this does not automatically prove every notification category in the new build. Show help/privacy/third-party notices once bundled in the new build.

11. **Account deletion and cleanup.** Finish media/report/block demonstrations before destructive tests. Delete the disposable non-owner first through `あなた → 設定 → 機種変更・データの保存 → アカウントの削除 → アカウントを削除` and the confirmation. Verify it returns to setup. The remaining owner can then delete their disposable account when no other members remain. If another member remains, demonstrate the ownership-transfer requirement first instead of trying to bypass it. Confirm only test accounts/family are affected. Keep the real family untouched.

12. **Evidence to retain.** When recording is resumed, retain the original physical-device video, build/device/time details, actual result of each step, and separate inbox/report-operation evidence as needed. Mark failed or untested steps explicitly. Give Apple the recording plus concise answers to purpose/audience, setup/credentials, external services, regional behavior and permissions. Do not say build 16 already contains the pending safety controls or claim email/safety steps succeeded before this run.
