1. Recording and account flows
Physical-device recording pending. Registration is described below. For login, first register an email in あなた → 設定 → 機種変更・データの保存 → メールを登録する. On another installation, choose メールでログイン and enter the six-digit emailed code. There is no fixed review password or universal code.
Deletion: あなた → 設定 → 機種変更・データの保存 → アカウントの削除 → アカウントを削除 → 完全に削除. An organizer with other members must first transfer ownership. Use a disposable account. There are no paid features or in-app purchases.
Build 16 reports other members' comments. General user blocking and reporting answers/attachments are being added for the next build; they are not present in build 16.

2. Purpose, audience and value
Tsutsuura is a free Japanese-language app for private family communication, including relatives living apart and older adults. One daily question gives members a simple reason to share memories and everyday experiences. Large controls, optional voice input and family-assisted setup support less confident smartphone users. Answers, photos/audio, likes and comments stay within the family; there is no public discovery feed.

3. Setup and main features
No existing account, password, email or phone verification is required. Choose 新しく家族をつくる, enter organizer/family names, tap この名前で次へ, draw a mark in あなたのしるし, then このしるしを使う → 使い方を見てはじめる. Notification permission is optional.
On home, tap 回答する for an available unanswered question, enter text, optionally add voice or up to four photos, and confirm sending. 家族 shows family answers; あなた shows your history/search. Tap photos for full-screen viewing or コメント to reply. For a second device, add a family member and generate a six-digit invitation; choose 家族の番号を入力 on that device. Codes expire after 10 minutes. No review credentials are needed for this self-serve flow.

4. Services and tools
This is a native Swift/SwiftUI app using Apple frameworks, including PhotosUI, Speech/AVFoundation and UserNotifications/APNs. Our PHP/SQLite API runs on ConoHa WING at https://toshizo.link/tsutsuura-api/api. Optional email recovery uses PHP mail; SMS is disabled. No third-party app SDK, advertising, payment or external social-login service is required. Xcode/GitHub Actions are development/build tools, not services users must join.

5. Regional behavior
The same features are offered in all available regions. The interface and questions are Japanese. Daily questions use Asia/Tokyo dates and appear between 09:00 and 19:00 Japan time; the app shows the publication time before answering becomes available. Notification quiet hours use the account's stored timezone. There are no region-specific feature modes.

6. Regulated services and protected material
The app provides no regulated financial, medical or gambling service and no third-party media catalogue. Family members supply their own responses and attachments.
